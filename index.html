Data Enrichment · Nonprofit · Ongoing Retainer
6,253-Contact Gala Invite List — Enriched, Scored, & Segmented
An Indiana Realtor running a major fundraising gala needed to turn a sprawling 6,253-contact list into an actionable outreach campaign. Contacts came from four different sources (Mailchimp exports, physical mail lists, direct entries) with inconsistent formats, duplicates, and missing data across email, phone, and address fields.

I built a full multi-stage enrichment pipeline: PDL (Person Data Labs) API for the 3,828 email-keyed contacts (63% match rate; 73% on pilot batch), Mailchimp deduplication adding 1,703 net new contacts, city-to-ZIP correction for 117 flagged Indiana records, and Tier 1 scoring by salary and job title. Final master: 6,253 rows across six tabs — email pool, Tier 1 high-value targets, Indiana segment, physical mail pool, and Greenvelope-ready upload.
